MANIFESTO

The Mediterranean
Soul of Architecture

Design in Barcelona is not an aesthetic trend; it is an enduring architectural legacy.

Born from the unique interplay between Mediterranean light, honest materials and decades of creative freedom, Catalan design is rooted in functional honesty and timeless elegance.

We bridge this rich tradition with contemporary architecture and interiors around the world, bringing authentic design from Barcelona to the spaces that define them.

BARCELONA ATELIER

Where Architectural Heritage
Meets Master Craftsmanship

Barcelona is a living atelier for exceptional design.

Beyond its world-renowned architectural heritage, the city is home to a deeply rooted network of specialised workshops, master cabinetmakers, glassblowers and skilled metalworkers.

Here, design is not simply made for mass production. Each piece is carefully crafted and refined through generations of technical knowledge and craftsmanship.

This unique ecosystem bridges artistic freedom and industrial rigour.

Across Catalonia, time-honoured Mediterranean techniques coexist with contemporary manufacturing processes, allowing designers and makers to explore complex forms, natural materials and new ways of working.

From established design icons to emerging makers and one-of-a-kind pieces, our collection reflects the creative culture of Barcelona and its surrounding territory.

HERITAGE TIMELINE

Three eras.
One living culture.

1900s

The Artisanal Dawn

Catalan Modernisme established the idea of the total work of art, bringing architecture together with master craftsmanship, bespoke interiors and organic forms. Barcelona's architecture became a meeting point between artistic vision and exceptional craftsmanship, with figures such as Antoni Gaudí and Josep Maria Jujol helping define this distinctive language.

1960s–1970s

A New Language of Design

Barcelona's creative culture evolved into a new language of modern design. Designers, architects and makers embraced functional clarity, honest materials and the Mediterranean way of living, creating pieces that remain relevant across generations.

Present

A Living Design Culture

Today, Barcelona continues to evolve through a new generation of designers, architects and makers. Traditional craftsmanship meets contemporary technology, independent studios coexist with established manufacturers, and new voices continue to reinterpret the Mediterranean spirit.

This living culture is the heritage we source from today.
